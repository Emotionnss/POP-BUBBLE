# Pop Bubble

> A simple pixel-art arcade game where a rice farmer jumps on bubbles, collects fruits, and tries to achieve the highest score!

## About the Game

**Pop Bubble** is a casual, endless arcade game featuring simple pixel-art graphics and easy-to-learn controls.

Play as a rice farmer who must stay afloat by jumping on bubbles while collecting fruits along the way. Keep moving, control your jumps, and avoid dangerous obstacles to survive for as long as possible!

The objective is simple: **Stay afloat, collect fruits, and achieve the highest score!**

## Gameplay

### Main Objective

Stay afloat by continuously jumping on bubbles while collecting fruits to increase your score.

The game features an endless scrolling environment where objects move from right to left. Players must react to incoming bubbles, fruits, and obstacles while maintaining their position in the air.

### How to Play

1. Start the game and let your character fall towards the incoming bubbles.
2. Land on bubbles to bounce back into the air.
3. Collect pixel-art fruits to earn additional points.
4. Control your movement and jumping height to avoid falling.
5. Survive for as long as possible to achieve a higher score.

### Game Over Conditions

The game ends when:

- **Falling:** The player misses the bubbles and falls off the playable area.
- **Bombs:** The player touches a bomb and loses the game.

## Controls

The game uses only two directional inputs, making it simple and accessible to play.

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Left Arrow | Move Left |
| Right Arrow | Move Right |

Players must use these two controls to position themselves, land on bubbles, and collect fruits.

## Game Mechanics

### 1. Bubble Jumping

Bubbles are the player's main way of staying afloat.

- The player starts by falling towards the bubbles.
- Landing on a bubble causes the player to bounce upwards.
- Missing a bubble can cause the player to fall.
- Players must carefully control their movement to land on incoming bubbles.

### 2. Endless Scrolling

The game features a continuously scrolling environment.

- The environment moves from right to left.
- New bubbles, fruits, and obstacles continuously appear.
- Players must react to incoming objects and adjust their movement accordingly.
- The game has no fixed ending as long as the player survives.

### 3. Jump Height & Movement

Controlling the player's position and jump height is an important part of the gameplay.

Players must figure out how to position themselves to land on bubbles while avoiding dangerous obstacles.

Good movement and timing are essential to surviving longer.

### 4. Fruit Collection

Fruits appear throughout the game and can be collected by the player.

Collecting fruits rewards additional points and encourages players to take risks while navigating the environment.

### 5. Bombs & Obstacles

Bombs are dangerous obstacles that the player must avoid.

Touching a bomb immediately ends the game, making awareness and careful movement important for survival.

---

## Scoring System

Players earn points through two main activities:

- **Survival Time:** The score increases as time passes and the player stays alive.
- **Fruit Collection:** Collecting fruits grants additional points.

The longer the player survives and the more fruits they collect, the higher their final score becomes.

The goal is to achieve the highest possible score before the game ends.

---

## Leaderboard

After a game ends, players can enter their name to submit their score to the leaderboard.

The leaderboard displays the highest-scoring players, allowing players to compare their results and compete for a place among the top five.

### Leaderboard Features

- Enter a player name after finishing a game.
- Submit the final score.
- Display the top 5 players.
- Compare scores with other players.
